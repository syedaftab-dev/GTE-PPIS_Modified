\section*{Key Caveats}
% ================================================================

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
    \item \textbf{Single seed only} (\code{SEED=2024}).
    Deltas of $\Delta$\,MCC $<$ 0.02 are within plausible seed noise.
    Multi-seed reruns ($\geq$3 seeds) required before any claim is publishable.

    \item \textbf{Run~2 never executed.}
    Loss-function contribution (res=False,$\,\gamma$=0.0) remains unmeasured.
    The Run~1$\to$Run~3 step confounds residual and loss changes.

    \item \textbf{UBtest split mismatch.}
    \texttt{UBtest\_31-6} $\neq$ paper's \texttt{UBtest\_25}; all UBtest figures are for
    internal fold-consistency tracking only.

    \item \textbf{Remaining gap sources (unresolved this session).}
    EGNN \code{tanh=True}/\code{attention=True}, \code{ReduceLROnPlateau} schedule,
    possible EGNN dim/layer count discrepancy, unknown paper seed.
\end{enumerate}
